They are concatenated here into one minified, fingerprinted request, so splitting the source costs the visitor nothing. TWO THINGS ARE LOAD-BEARING: 1. `sort ... "Name"`. resources.Match returns the PROJECT's files first and the MODULE's second, NOT in filename order. Without this sort a consumer's 61-component-gallery.css would load before the theme's 00-tokens.css and the cascade would invert. Verified, not theoretical. 2. The numeric prefixes. They are the cascade order, which is why they step in 5s and 10s — there is room to insert without renaming. A consumer overrides any single file by putting a file of the same name in their own assets/css/pihuw/. Hugo's union filesystem gives theirs precedence, and the sort keeps it in the theme's cascade position. A new file, e.g. 95-mine.css, simply joins the bundle at its sorted place. */ -}} {{- with sort (resources.Match "css/pihuw/*.css") "Name" -}} {{- partial "tmpl/css-link" (dict "res" (. | resources.Concat "css/pihuw.bundle.css")) -}} {{- end -}} {{- /* Pre-v0.5 sites kept their overrides in assets/css/pihuw.css, which the theme no longer loads.
